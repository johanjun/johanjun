## KQ1. 본업과 병행 가능성: 원격 운영이 되는 모델과 자주 현장에 가야 하는 모델

### Takeaway
None of the offline models is truly "passive." Each depends on restocking, cleaning, machine faults, theft or noise complaints, and guest check-ins. The ones that fit a full-time job best rely on remote kiosks or CCTV and outsourced cleaning: self photo booths, coin laundries, vending machines, and short-term rentals with an outside manager. Some models need frequent or unpredictable in-person attention: unmanned cafés and ice-cream shops (stock and theft), party rooms (cleaning after every booking, late-night noise), and 외국인관광 도시민박업 (the host must legally live in the home). The weekend labor gigs need no capital, but income stops when the hours stop.

### Cited Findings
- 무인점포 "무인" is a misnomer in practice. A news feature on people who spent their 퇴직금 on an unmanned shop found that theft, upkeep and weak sales effectively forced owners to be on site (주인 상주 필요) — [Daum/뉴스 "창업은 쉽지만 버티긴 힘들다…퇴직금 털어 무인 창업했다 '후회만'"](https://v.daum.net/v/1v9BlQ3IMa)
- Unmanned stores have nobody present, so they are directly exposed to theft and vandalism (절도나 기물 파손). Toss Place, a POS company, publishes a security guide for owners for this reason — [토스플레이스 무인매장 운영 가이드](https://tossplace.com/story/unmannedstore_operation) **[vendor content]**
- Party room, anecdotal: a seller listing a Gwangjin-gu party room says it made about 300만원/월 net during three peak months, while the seller "had a main job and barely paid attention." This is an anecdotal sales pitch — [당근 부동산 매물 (광진구)](https://realty.daangn.com/articles/2343620) **[anecdotal, sales-motivated]**
- Party room, anecdotal: an Uijeongbu party room listing reports annual revenue of about 1,000–1,200만원 and a best month of about 223만원 (excluding bank transfers). Fixed costs besides rent were about 3만원 plus 2–4만원 in electricity per month — [당근 부동산 매물 (의정부)](https://realty.daangn.com/articles/3775738) **[anecdotal]**
- 외국인관광 도시민박업 legally requires the operator to live in the home (실거주, with 전입신고). A space separate from the operator's own residence cannot be registered — [에어비앤비 외도민 안내 PDF (2025-07)](https://news.airbnb.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2025/07/%EC%99%B8%EB%8F%84%EB%AF%BC.pdf); [wikidocs 정리 (2026)](https://wikidocs.net/blog/@history/21294/) **[blog, low reliability]**
- Self photo booths usually run 2–3 kiosks in a space of about 10평 — [비즈한국 2025-06](https://bizhankook.com/articles/29706.html). In a 2023 Seongsu-dong scenario, operating costs were film and electricity (about 150만원/월 combined with royalty) plus rent, so day-to-day labor is light — [매경이코노미 via Daum 2023-03](https://v.daum.net/v/20230309223909652)
- Vending machines: location (입지) drives most of the result, and good sites are places where people stay put, such as office pantries, factory break rooms and university libraries — [hansungshowcase 블로그](https://blog.hansungshowcase.kr/coffee-vending-machine-installation-guide) **[홍보성]**; [비즈한국](https://bizhankook.com/articles/23414.html)
- Study café franchises claim their unmanned systems cut labor costs by 80% or more. This is a franchisee or brand claim — [엑스포츠뉴스 2026-08](https://www.xportsnews.com/article/1239234) **[홍보성]**
- Short-term rentals (삼삼엠투): the operator says landlords fill vacancies in 3 days on average (company figure). A 서울디지털대 professor warned that sublet (전대) structures leave liability unclear when legal problems arise — [유니콘팩토리 2025-01](https://www.unicornfactory.co.kr/article/2025012310104023124); [머니투데이 2024-07](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2024/07/11/2024071008572316961)
- Delivery and gig baseline: in the 2017 coverage, 대리운전 drivers who work two or three jobs and drive only short stints, or only on weekends, are common — [노컷뉴스](https://www.nocutnews.co.kr/news/4874240)

### Inferences
- **Compatibility tiers.** The hour figures below are inferred from how each model operates. No source quantified hours per week.
  - **High (an outside manager or remote operation is realistic):** short-term rentals or 단기임대 through 삼삼엠투 or 리브애니웨어 with a hired cleaner; self photo booths, which need film and paper refills and kiosk checks perhaps weekly; coin laundries, which need detergent, change and fault calls; and vending machines placed in someone else's premises.
  - **Medium:** study cafés, which run on kiosks and CCTV but generate a steady stream of complaints about noise, seats and temperature, plus daily cleaning that must be outsourced. Unmanned ice-cream and snack shops need restocking several times a week, expiry and freezer checks, and theft follow-up with police reports and CCTV review.
  - **Low (hard to fit around a 9-to-6 job):** party rooms, which need a turnover clean after every booking and get late-night noise complaints from neighbors. Unmanned cafés need daily machine cleaning and milk and consumables handling. 외국인관광 도시민박업 requires living in the home and doing foreign-language guest service. Claw-machine shops need prize restocking and machine payout tuning, which is now under regulatory scrutiny.
- "Emergency calls" are the hidden cost in the unmanned formats. A failed kiosk payment, a door-lock fault, a freezer failure or a theft alert can arrive during work hours, and someone has to go to the store. Theft reports grew roughly threefold over 2021–2023 (see KQ2), so this is a structural risk, not a rare one.
- Weekend labor gigs (배달, 쿠팡플렉스, 대리운전, 과외) have the most predictable earnings per hour. They need no capital beyond a vehicle or license, but they only pay while the person is working.

### Gaps
- No survey or official source gives hours per week, or the frequency of emergency visits, for any of the unmanned formats. The tiering above is my inference.
- Outsourced cleaning and management costs (청소대행, 위탁운영 fees) for party rooms, Airbnb-type stays and study cafés were not found in reliable sources.

## KQ2. 모델별 수익·비용·폐업 데이터 (2025–2026, 신뢰도 표시)

### Takeaway
The official data are dominated by a broad small-business downturn. In 2024, 1,008,282 businesses closed (the first year above 1 million, 폐업률 9.04%). The average small-business tenant's operating profit fell to 4,800만원 a year. Every heavily promoted unmanned format shows the same boom-then-saturation pattern: 무인 밀키트 in 2021–23, 무인 아이스크림 from 2021 onward, self photo booths from 2022 onward (closures up 3.5x from 2020 to 2024), and study cafés (62x more card merchants from 2015 to 2024). Reliable per-store profit data are almost nonexistent. Most revenue and profit figures come from franchise or consulting blogs. Where they exist, they suggest that a typical (median) unmanned store nets somewhere between a loss and about 100–200만원 a month on 3천만–2억원 of capital. Payback is multi-year, and equipment loses most of its value quickly.

The closure rate was 9.04%, and 50.2% of closures cited 사업 부진 (weak business) — [세계일보 2025-07-06](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20250706503716); [헤럴드경제](https://heraldk.com/?p=2576628)

- The 2025 상가건물임대차 실태조사 (중기부·소진공, published 2026-05-17, covering 2024 results) found that small-business tenants averaged **4,800만원** in annual operating profit, down from 8,200만원 in the previous survey. Average rent was 112만원/월, average revenue 2.12억원, and the average debt balance 1.44억원 — [한경비즈니스 2026-05](https://magazine.hankyung.com/business/article/202605171652b); [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000310846)

Opening and closure rates are as of end-2024. The service-sector breakdown was not visible in the search summaries — [뉴시스 2026-04-11](https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20260411_0003587198); [서울시 경제 누리집](https://news.seoul.go.kr/economy/archives/572148)

### Inferences
- **Payback and return math, computed from the cited figures (illustrative):**
  - 무인카페: 5,500–8,000만원 invested. A middle-tier store does 300–450만원/월 in revenue. Subtracting 20–30% cost of goods, 50–130만원 rent, 15–20만원 utilities, and card and POS fees leaves about 50–200만원/월 net before the owner's time. Payback is roughly **3–10+ years**. The bottom tier, which the promotional source itself calls loss-making, never pays back.
  - 셀프사진관: a new kiosk costs over 1,000만원 but sells for 200–300만원 used within a year. **On exit, only about 20–30% of the equipment cost is recovered.** That is the single clearest quantified downside in this research. Aggregate revenue still grew in 2024 (+10%, from 2,906억원 to 3,196억원) while closures rose 19% (514 → 611), which looks like a market moving from growth into churn.
  - 스터디카페 (about 2억원) and 코인빨래방 (about 1억원 or more) sit well above the 3천만원 tier and are not "modest side-income" bets. A loss means losing years of salary savings.
- The study-café merchant count rose from 112 to 6,944 while the student population shrank. Demand is migrating to adult learners, whose usage is less intensive than exam students'. Brands now discount franchise fees to recruit owners. Together these suggest the supply side is saturated, although I found no closure statistics specific to study cafés.
- The pattern across 밀키트, 아이스크림, 셀프사진관 and (probably) 스터디카페 is consistent. A format becomes fashionable, franchisors push expansion, stores cluster within a few hundred meters, and per-store revenue collapses within 1–3 years. A 회사원 who enters a format *because it is being heavily advertised in 2026* is likely entering late.
- The best-documented "sure money per hour" options are 과외 (about 3–5.5만원/시간 if the person has the credentials) and 배달 (about 1.3만원/시간 for 커넥터, per a company survey, before vehicle costs). 쿠팡플렉스 can fall to around minimum wage or below after vehicle costs, based on 2020–21 data. All of these carry essentially no capital at risk.

Search summaries did not expose 업종-level 평균매출 or 폐점률 for 스터디카페, 무인 아이스크림, 셀프사진관 or 코인빨래방.

- No reliable source gives party-room, practice-room or studio net profit. Only listings and franchise claims are available.
- No verified 2025–26 쿠팡플렉스 rates, 대리운전 income or 쿠팡이츠 2026 per-delivery fees were found.

## KQ3. 규제·법적 리스크 (숙박, 소방·위생, 임대차, 주거지역 용도, 보험)

### Takeaway
Accommodation is the most regulated and most changed area. Airbnb has removed unregistered listings: new listings had to be registered from Oct 2024, existing ones from 2025-10-16, and unregistered listings cannot take bookings for stays from 2026-01-01. 외국인관광 도시민박업 requires the host to live in the home, excludes officetels, and is limited to foreign guests. Since 2026-08-04, a single failure to post or honor room rates means a business suspension. The 위홈 "A-type" 내국인 sandbox stopped issuing new permits after 2026-07-31. Legalizing domestic-guest sharing is being discussed but has not been enacted. Short-term rental (단기임대) is legal as leasing, which is why it is booming, but tenants fall outside the 주택임대차보호법 and subletting carries liability risk. Party rooms sit in a gray zone: they are hard to police as space rental but are illegal if they host overnight sleeping. P2P private-car sharing is essentially illegal outside sandbox pilots.

### Cited Findings
**숙박 공유 (에어비앤비·외도민)**
- Airbnb required business registration information for new listings from October 2024 and applied the requirement to all existing listings from **2025-10-16, 08:00 KST**. Listings without a registration certificate **cannot accept bookings for stays from 2026-01-01**. They can resume after registering. Existing confirmed bookings were not auto-cancelled. Airbnb says the measure is voluntary, not a legal obligation on platforms — [Airbnb 뉴스룸](https://news.airbnb.com/ko/enforcement); [뉴스와이어](https://www.newswire.co.kr/newsRead.php?no=1016951); [한국경제 2025-09-02](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202509027541K)
- Officetel listings, which cannot obtain a registration, were to be removed — [Daum 2024-09-24](https://v.daum.net/v/20240924131813327)
- "Balloon effect": unregistered operators removed from Airbnb have moved to other platforms and keep operating, and government enforcement is inconsistent — [Daum 단독 2025-09-23](https://v.daum.net/v/20250923060212141)
- 외국인관광 도시민박업 requirements: a house in an urban area (officetels and urban studio-type housing are excluded); the operator must actually live there (전입신고); total floor area under 230㎡; neighbor consent where apartment bylaws require it, and the scope of consent varies by local government; foreign guests only; fire extinguishers, a standalone smoke alarm in each room and a CO alarm with individual heating; foreign-language guidance — [Airbnb 외도민 PDF 2025-07](https://news.airbnb.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2025/07/%EC%99%B8%EB%8F%84%EB%AF%BC.pdf); [서울시 (2019 version)](https://news.seoul.go.kr/culture/?p=27478)
- **Overcharging penalties:** an amended Tourism Promotion Act enforcement decree, effective **2026-08-04**, suspends 외국인관광 도시민박업 and 한옥체험업 operators for failing to post room rates or charging above the posted rate. Suspension is 5 days for the first offense, 15 days for the second and 1 month for the third — [뉴시스 2026-07-28](https://mobile.newsis.com/view/NISX20260728_0003726604); [데일리안](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1671828/); [문화체육관광부](https://www.mcst.go.kr/site/s_notice/press/pressView.jsp?pSeq=22610)
- Enforcement in 2026: an inspection of 12 sites for domestic-guest use and fire equipment led to administrative action against 2 — [세계일보 2026-06-29](https://www.segye.com/newsView/20260629518748). Seoul ran a special crackdown on lodging violations before a BTS concert — [Korea Times 2026-03-08](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/southkorea/society/20260308/seoul-launches-crackdown-on-lodging-violations-ahead-of-bts-concert)
- **Domestic guests (내국인):** Airbnb's help page says new A-type sandbox permits (위홈) ended on **2026-07-31**, existing A-type permits remain valid until **2027-12-31**, and permits cannot be transferred. B-type (a registered 외국인관광 도시민박업 host also serving domestic guests) is unchanged — [Airbnb 도움말](https://www.airbnb.co.kr/e/ppap_sub9) *(single source)*. 위홈 was reportedly converted from a demonstration exemption to an ICT sandbox temporary permit on 2026-05-07 — [나무위키](https://namu.moe/w/%EC%9C%84%ED%99%88) **[low reliability]**. Seoul proposed in Dec 2025 that 도시민박 be opened to domestic guests — [머니투데이 2025-12-10](https://www.mt.co.kr/society/2025/12/10/2025121008273493421). Legislation to allow domestic guests in shared lodging was "to gain momentum next year" (date unclear) — [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000279641). Coverage of a possible 'K-스테이' market — [디지털데일리 2026-03-12](https://www.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2026031218023064420)
- Residential buildings (multi-family houses, apartments, commercial buildings, officetels) cannot be registered as lodging businesses. Unregistered lodging can lead to closure, or up to 2 years' imprisonment or a fine of up to 2,000만원 (2022 article) — see the 파티룸 search summary; source article in the [Asiae 2022-07 piece on unauthorized lodging](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2022072114255827654) *(verify the exact statute: 공중위생관리법)*

**단기임대 (삼삼엠투·리브애니웨어)**
- Using a house or officetel for lodging is illegal, but leasing it is allowed regardless of property type. That legal difference is the stated reason short-term rental platforms grew as illegal-lodging enforcement tightened. Short-term rentals are not covered by the **주택임대차보호법**, so tenant protections are weak — [파이낸셜뉴스 2025-02-17](https://www.fnnews.com/news/202502171844359071)
- In subletting (전대), it is unclear who bears responsibility when legal problems arise — [머니투데이 2024-07](https://www.mt.co.kr/future/2024/07/11/2024071008572316961)

**공간대여 (파티룸 등)**
- Mapo-gu office: party rooms count as space rental, not lodging, so "there is no regulation to enforce." Police respond to noise complaints, but punishment is rare. Residents near Hongdae complain of party rooms in residential one-rooms — [위키트리](https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/352040)
- Gyeongnam: 11 party rooms or "space rentals" that offered all-night packages or sold overnight stays on Airbnb were referred to prosecutors under the 공중위생관리법. - Converting a house into a party room triggers fire-safety standards for the new use, so consult the local fire station in advance. Opening before the completion inspection certificate can bring a fine, and the 다중이용업소법 may apply depending on size — [bznav AI 콘텐츠](https://ai.bznav.com/contents/3083694) **[low reliability; verify with 소방서]**. **스터디카페**
- In Feb 2023 the Supreme Court ruled that a study café is not a 독서실, so it need not register under the 학원법 — [Daum/연합 2023-02-26](https://v.daum.net/v/20230226154500271)

**차량 공유**

P2P car sharing is considered illegal under that law — [시대 2022-08](https://www.sidae.com/article/2022082410020914522); [보험연구원 KIRI 보고서](https://www.kiri.or.kr/report/downloadFile.do?docId=2304)

**자판기·인형뽑기**
- Food vending: machines selling tea-type drinks or processed foods such as noodles must be reported to the district office. Machines selling sealed finished products such as canned drinks are exempt — [비즈한국](https://bizhankook.com/articles/23414.html); [레이디경향](https://lady.khan.co.kr/finance/article/8210) *(search summary blended these two)*
- Claw machines: the game rating agency is tightening checks on prize types and payout standards — [문화일보](https://www.munhwa.com/article/11016577)

**세금·건강보험 (직장인 특화)**
- An employee whose non-salary income (business, rental, interest and so on) exceeds **2,000만원 a year** pays an extra 소득월액 health-insurance premium on the excess. The threshold was tightened in Sept 2022. About 804,951 employees paid on average about 15.2만원/월 extra — [SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000219890); [asiatop](https://asiatop.co.kr/insurance-labor/salaried-extra-income-health-insurance/). A proposal to cut the threshold to 1,000만원 was reported to a 건정심 subcommittee, but its status is unconfirmed — [의약일보](https://medicaldaily.co.kr/news/21462)

### Inferences
- For a 회사원 without a home in a tourist area, the 2025–26 tightening makes Airbnb-style hosting impractical. Officetels are out. Separate units are illegal unless registered under a different lodging category, which residential buildings mostly cannot obtain. 외국인관광 도시민박업 requires living on site, foreign guests only, and now carries a one-strike suspension for rate violations. Legal domestic-guest sharing remains a prospect, not a reality, as of Nov 2026.
- 단기임대 is currently the legal "path of least resistance" for monetizing a spare or owned unit. It relies on lease contracts, which lack 주임법 protection on either side, and on the platform's terms. Subletting a unit the person rents needs explicit landlord consent.
- The main legal risk for party rooms in residential areas comes from complaints. Noise complaints, an "all-night" package that turns the room into de facto lodging, fire-code compliance after a change of use, and building use classification (근린생활시설 vs. 주택) can all trigger it. Check the 건축물대장 use classification and fire requirements before signing a lease.
- Insurance: no source detailed liability or property insurance for unmanned stores or party rooms. Given the theft trend and fire-safety blind spots, a 회사원 should price 화재·영업배상책임보험 into fixed costs (gap below).
- Personal car sharing is not a viable legal side business. Any paid lending of a private car outside a sandbox would likely breach the transport law and void ordinary auto insurance. The insurance point is inferred and not verified in a source.

### Gaps

- The current enactment status of legal domestic-guest shared lodging (관광진흥법 amendment) after Nov 2026 is unclear. Primary sources (국회 의안정보, 문체부) were not fetched.

## KQ4. 자본 규모별(0원 / 500만원 / 3천만원) 리스크 조정 수익 비교, 온라인 대비

### Takeaway
At **0원**, the only realistic offline options are labor gigs, with stable but capped income per hour: tutoring at about 3–5만원/시간 for qualified people, delivery at about 1.3만원/시간 for part-time riders before costs, 대리운전, and 쿠팡플렉스 (often near minimum wage after vehicle costs). Short-term renting an already-owned or vacant unit is a second option. At **500만원**, the money buys only small equipment bets: vending machines, a used photo kiosk at 200–300만원, or camping gear. Each needs a host location and has weak, location-dependent returns. At **3천만원**, the money reaches only the bottom of the unmanned-store market (minimal unmanned café, small unmanned shop). In saturated 2025–26 markets the median outcome is roughly break-even to about 100만원/월, with a real risk of losing most of the capital, because equipment resells at a fraction of cost and closing costs average about 2,000만원. On risk-adjusted terms, most offline space businesses look worse for a salaried worker than low-capital online options or simply keeping the money, unless the person already controls a cheap space or a good location.

- **Risk-adjusted view.** The 3천만원 tier involves a multi-year payback, equipment that resells at 20–30% of cost, average closing costs around 2,000만원, lease lock-in, theft exposure and saturated markets. Its expected return-to-risk ratio is poor compared with online models that have near-zero capital and low fixed costs, which another researcher covers. In those online models the worst case is wasted time, not a 2,000–5,000만원 loss. A reasonable benchmark for 3천만원 is a deposit account. At an *assumed* ~3% rate (not verified for 2026), that is about 7.5만원/월 before tax with no risk. An unmanned store must beat this by enough to justify the time spent, emergency calls and tail risk.
- **Where offline can still make sense:** (1) the person already controls a cheap or vacant space, such as an owned unit, a family-owned commercial ground floor or a company break room for a vending machine, so the rent and deposit risk disappear; (2) they have a skill premium (tutoring); or (3) they treat a small equipment bet (for example, a used photo kiosk inside an existing business) as an add-on rather than a stand-alone store.
- Income in the range of 수십만~200만원/월 is reachable offline mostly through (a) weekend labor at 10–20 hours a week, or (b) a successful, well-located store run with outsourced cleaning. Option (b) requires capital whose downside is several years of savings.
- Tax and health-insurance drag: if side-business net income plus other non-salary income exceeds 2,000만원 a year, the extra 소득월액 premium applies, so a 200만원/월 target (about 2,400만원/년) crosses the threshold.
